Where electric wins

The case for the Flow

  • Silent, generator-free power for up to a week off-grid
  • No propane to refill, monitor, or store
  • Self-hitching and remote parking remove the hardest chores
  • Tow assist can offset range/MPG lost to towing
  • Doubles as a giant battery for your home or EV
  • Over-the-air updates keep adding features
Where gas still wins

The honest trade-offs

  • Much lower entry price on conventional trailers
  • Refueling is minutes; charging is a longer stop
  • Mature dealer and service network nationwide
  • Proven, well-understood long-term reliability
  • More floor plans, sizes and price points to choose from
  • No charging logistics to plan around on long hauls

Bottom line. The Flow trades a higher sticker price and some charging planning for near-zero running fuel costs, silence, and a dramatically easier setup.
