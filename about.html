About this site

Made by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts

PebbleFlowRV.com is an independent fan community documenting the all-electric Pebble Flow travel trailer — specs, features, guides, videos and owner stories, gathered in one clean, easy-to-scan place.

Disclaimer

PebbleFlowRV.com is an independent fan site and is not affiliated with, authorized by, sponsored by, or endorsed by Pebble Mobility Inc. “Pebble,” “Pebble Flow,” and related names and logos are trademarks of Pebble Mobility Inc. We use them only for identification and commentary. For official information, pricing and orders, always visit pebblelife.com.

All specifications, prices and figures on this site are gathered from publicly available materials and are provided for general information only. Details change — especially on a product that updates over the air — so treat Pebble’s official channels as the source of truth. Nothing here is professional, financial, or purchasing advice.

Our content policy

We take copyright and attribution seriously. On this site we aim to:

  • Use our own words, analysis and original graphics rather than copying Pebble’s pages.
  • Link to official sources and quote only short, attributed excerpts for commentary.
  • Publish photos that are our own, licensed, or submitted by owners with permission — not re-hosted marketing images.
  • Embed videos only when the creator allows embedding, and always credit the original creator.

See something that needs correcting or attributing differently? Let us know and we’ll fix it promptly.

Contribute

Owner photos, road-trip reports, real energy numbers and honest reviews are the heart of a good fan site. If you own or have ridden in a Flow, we’d love to feature your story (with credit, and only with your permission).

Get in touch

Email us at hello@pebbleflowrv.com with photos, stories, or corrections. Tell us how you’d like to be credited.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. Ad placements are clearly labeled and do not influence our editorial content or opinions. We aren’t paid by Pebble, and featuring the Flow is not an endorsement deal — we’re just fans.
