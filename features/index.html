The good stuff

Every Pebble Flow feature, explained

The Flow bundles a set of genuinely new-to-RVing tricks — most unlocked by the dual-motor Magic Pack. Here’s what each one actually does, and why it matters on a real trip.

Leaderboard ad · 970×90
Magic Pack

Magic Hitch

Hitching a trailer usually means a spotter, a lot of tiny reversing, and some choice words. The Flow uses cameras and AI to sight your tow vehicle, align itself, and connect automatically. You pull up, tap once, and it does the fiddly part on its own.

Magic Pack

Remote Control & 360° turn

Because the Flow has its own dual-motor drivetrain, the Pebble App can reverse, park and position it without you touching the tow vehicle. It can even rotate a full 360° in place — priceless for tight campsites and driveways where backing a trailer is nearly impossible.

One-tap setup

InstaCamp

Arriving and leaving are the two most tedious moments of any camping trip. InstaCamp collapses them into a button: it auto-levels, unfolds the stairs, turns on the lights, sets the temperature and unlocks the door. Retracting camp is just as quick when you head home.

Magic Pack

Easy Tow & Active Tow Assist

The motors don’t just self-park — they push while you drive. Active Tow Assist eases the load on your SUV or truck, improves stability in crosswinds and on grades, and can boost gas mileage or extend EV range. Recharge Mode even uses regen braking to top the battery back up en route.

In-content ad · 728×90

And the rest

Living, power & the smart layer

Auto Dump

A hygienic, nearly touch-less system for emptying the grey and black tanks — the chore no one misses.

The Pebble App

Control temperature, lighting, battery, water levels, Magic Hitch and Remote Control. Works off-grid over a direct local connection — no internet required.

Solar + 45 kWh battery

Optional 1.1 kW roof solar keeps the LFP pack topped off for up to seven days off-grid — no propane, no generator.

12 kW power export

Roughly 3.5× a Powerwall of capacity, out through a NEMA 14-50 outlet — run a campsite, charge an EV, or back up your home.

Chef’s kitchen

A removable indoor/outdoor induction “Wandering Cooktop,” 4-in-1 convection microwave, full-size fridge and a pass-through service window.

Room Flip & Starlink-ready

The queen Murphy bed converts to a work-from-anywhere office with power, task lighting and plug-and-play Starlink connectivity.

Note on the Magic Pack. Remote Control, Magic Hitch, Easy Tow and Auto Dump depend on the dual-motor Magic Pack. The Flow Standard model is expected to omit the motor pack, so confirm which features are included with the trim you’re considering.
