What it costs

Trims & pricing

Three configurations, from the self-propelled flagship down to a more attainable standard model. Financing figures are illustrative and depend on credit and terms — always confirm current pricing on Pebble’s order page.

Founders Edition

$175,000

The fully-loaded launch edition. Includes the dual-motor Magic Pack and the complete feature set. Delivering now.

Magic Pack · popular

$135,500

Adds the dual-motor drivetrain that unlocks Remote Control, Magic Hitch, Easy Tow and Auto Dump. Delivering now.

Flow Standard

$109,500

The more attainable model without the motor pack. Announced starting price; shipping later. Confirm availability with Pebble.

Financing. Pebble works with third-party RV lenders offering terms up to 20 years. Per Pebble, qualified buyers with 20% down and excellent credit can see payments starting around $910–$1,200/month. This is not financial advice — verify current rates and terms directly with the lender.

Warranty coverage
