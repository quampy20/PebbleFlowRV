Privacy policy

This policy explains what limited information this website collects and how third-party services — including advertising and analytics — may use cookies.

Information we collect

PebbleFlowRV.com is a content website. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to read our articles. If you voluntarily submit your email address to our newsletter, or contact us with photos or stories, we use that information only to respond to you or to send the updates you requested.

Cookies & advertising

We use advertising to support this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand which articles are popular and improve the site. Any analytics provider we use may set cookies or collect aggregated, non-identifying usage data.

Our articles link to external sites (including Pebble’s official pages and video platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Please review their policies separately.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email. For any privacy questions or requests, contact hello@pebbleflowrv.com.
